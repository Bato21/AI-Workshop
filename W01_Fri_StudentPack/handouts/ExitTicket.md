# Exit Ticket · Week 1 · Friday

This short reflection takes less than 5 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers: an honest account of what is clear or still confusing helps the instructor support your learning. Responses are visible only to the instructor and are used to improve the course, not to grade the correctness of your answers.

Answer all three questions in English. Write at least one complete sentence per question. If something is unclear, describe the uncertainty specifically rather than leaving the answer blank.

1. **What does one row represent in the results table and in the lap table?** Explain the difference in your own words, or identify the part you still find confusing.
2. **For a prediction made just before a race, which field would you avoid using and why?** Use an example from today's tables; if you are unsure, explain what you would need to check.
3. **What is your next concrete action for Lab 0, and when will you do it?** Include one remaining blocker or state that you have none; mention whether you worked with live data, a provided snapshot or synthetic data today.

If you need clarification or the ordinary submission route is inaccessible, contact the instructor privately. Keep your response personal; do not have an AI write a reflection about experiences it did not have.
