B. Check a claim made by AI · 12:25–12:35

How to read the curves: Horizontal = number of training rows. Vertical = accuracy. Training uses data the model saw; validation uses separate data. A gap is an observation; its cause is a hypothesis. Example: at 800 rows, Curve A has 65% training and 62% validation accuracy, a gap of 3 percentage points. The curves are fictional and say nothing about Baku or Sepang.

Use three checks: Form — does it separate observation from advice? Content — does the graph support its claims? Utility — is the next check specific and safe?
